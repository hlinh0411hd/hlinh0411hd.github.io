About the App

Learn Chinese. Grow your garden.

Chinese Garden combines structured Mandarin Chinese lessons, interactive exercises, and a peaceful little garden to make language learning enjoyable and rewarding at your own pace.

  • ✳ Step-by-step vocabulary with Pinyin and grammar inspired by HSK levels
  • ✳ Interactive review exercises, daily quests, and weekly rewards
  • ✳ Grow plants, collect seeds, and nurture your own Chinese garden
